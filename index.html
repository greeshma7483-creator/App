      How to Publish from an Android Phone (Free)

      No Laptop or Terminal Required! This file is 100% self-contained. It contains all HTML, CSS, JavaScript, questions, and features. Anyone with your public link can open it in any phone or computer browser without signing into Google AI Studio.
      Method 1: Tiiny.host (Fastest — 1 Minute from Mobile)
      1. Have your index.html file ready on your phone or computer.
      2. Open Chrome on your phone and go to tiiny.host.
      3. Upload your index.html file.
      4. Type any link name you like (e.g. interviewready-yourname) and tap Launch!
      5. Share the instant public URL with your lecturer, classmates, and friends.
      Method 2: GitHub Pages (Permanent & Professional)
      1. Open github.com in your phone's browser.
      2. Create a new repository called interviewready (set to Public).
      3. Tap "Add file" → "Upload files" and select index.html.
      4. Commit the file.
      5. Go to Settings → Pages, choose the main branch, and tap Save.
      6. In 60 seconds, GitHub gives you a permanent public URL: https://yourusername.github.io/interviewready/!
